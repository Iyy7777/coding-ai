Privacy Policy

Last updated: [Date]

1. What we collect

  • Account information: the email address and password you provide at sign-up (your password is stored as a secure hash by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store your plaintext password).
  • Chat content: the messages you send and the AI-generated replies, so your conversations can be saved and revisited.
  • Basic technical data: timestamps of activity, and standard web server logs (e.g. IP address, browser type) generated automatically by the hosting/auth infrastructure.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information (e.g. health, financial, or government ID data), and you should avoid entering such information into chat messages.

2. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account, and let you log in.
  • To store and display your chat history so you can pick up past conversations.
  • To send your messages to the AI model in order to generate replies.
  • To maintain and improve the App (e.g. diagnosing errors).

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who we share data with

We use the following third-party processors to operate the App:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles authentication (account creation, login, password storage).
  • AI model provider — depending on how this instance is configured, your messages may be processed by a locally run model (which never leaves the operator's own machine) or by a third-party hosted AI API, solely to generate a response to your message.

We do not share your data with advertisers, and we do not use your chats to train AI models ourselves.

4. Data retention

We retain your account and chat history for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual chats or your entire account at any time (see Section 6); deleted data is removed from our active database, subject to any residual copies in routine backups, which are purged on a rolling schedule.

5. Security

We rely on our infrastructure providers' security controls (encryption in transit via HTTPS, access controls, and Row Level Security policies restricting each account to its own data). No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information you transmit.

6. Your rights and choices

  • You can access and review your chat history at any time by logging in.
  • You can request correction of inaccurate account information.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting [contact email].
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR, CCPA, or the Australian Privacy Act — contact us to exercise them.

7. Cookies and local storage

The App uses browser local storage (via our authentication provider) to keep you logged in between visits. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or trackers.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction) without appropriate parental/guardian consent. If you believe a child has created an account without appropriate consent, contact us so we can remove it.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
